===============================================================================
1. DASHBOARD STORY (the narrative arc)
===============================================================================

Audience (per docs/problem_statement.md): a FIFA tournament strategy
analyst deciding whether the 48-team format should be retained, adjusted,
or reconsidered.

Core question: Did expanding to 48 teams increase representation *without*
materially degrading competitive balance or scheduling fairness?

Stance: the answer is allowed to be mixed — representation gains and
competitive-balance costs aren't mutually exclusive. Every visual must
support "associated with" language, never a causal verdict.

Arc across the report pages:

1. Frame the trade-off — 48 teams is a real, large jump in representation
   (24 -> 32 -> 48). State that plainly first.
2. Test the "did it get worse" question three ways — closeness of
   matches, upset frequency, ranking-vs-finish correlation. All three
   currently say no meaningful degradation.
3. Check draw fairness — is the group stage itself lopsided by design
   (2026 only; no historical baseline exists for this one).
4. Surface the real cost expansion did introduce — travel/rest burden,
   which isn't a "did it get worse" question at all, it's a "what did we
   trade for representation" question. This is the honest complication in
   an otherwise clean story.
5. Close on confidence — every number on the dashboard traces to a stated
   hypothesis, test, effect size, and sample size; nothing is asserted
   without its caveat visible.

3. DIMENSIONAL MODEL POWER BI CONNECTS TO
===============================================================================

Power BI queries the SHARED schema's 8 secure views (thin wrappers over
ANALYTICS, per Build 9) — not CORE directly. Each mart above is
effectively a pre-aggregated flat table, not something requiring further
joins in Power BI itself. The underlying star schema they're built from
(for context, not something PBI re-queries):

CORE.FACT_MATCH (220 rows: 104 2026 + 64 2022 + 52 1994)
 |- DIM_TOURNAMENT (3: 2026/2022/1994 - team_count, format_label)
 |- DIM_DATE (99)
 |- DIM_STAGE (7 - 2026 only; NULL for 2022/1994 rows)
 |- DIM_VENUE (16 - 2026 only; NULL for 2022/1994 rows)
 |- DIM_TEAM (62) --- DIM_GROUP (12, 2026-only)
 |                \-- DIM_CONFEDERATION (6, all 3 tournaments)
 \- TEAM_TOURNAMENT_RANKING (104: team x tournament, fifa_ranking)

Power BI model shape: one table per mart, loaded flat (Import or
DirectQuery against SHARED). No relationships need to be built between
marts in Power BI itself — each is already grain-correct and
self-contained. tournament_year is the one common column across marts
1-4 and 6, useful as a slicer field but not a formal relationship key,
since row grains differ (tournament vs. tournament x confederation vs.
tournament x team).

4. REPORT PAGES AND VISUALS
===============================================================================

--- Page 1 — Overview: The Expansion, By The Numbers ---
Story beat: ground the viewer in what actually changed before any
judgment.
- KPI cards: team_count per tournament (24 -> 32 -> 48), match_count
  (52 -> 64 -> 104)
- Table/bar: TOURNAMENT_FORMAT_COMPARISON — avg_goals_per_match,
  pct_one_goal_margin, pct_drawn_after_et, shootout_decided_matches, side
  by side across 3 tournaments
- Caveat visible on page: format_label makes clear these are structurally
  different tournaments (24/32/48-team), not the same format at
  different sizes

--- Page 2 — Competitive Balance ---
Story beat: are matches getting more lopsided?
- Grouped bar: avg_abs_goal_diff per tournament (COMPETITIVE_BALANCE)
- Bar: blowout_rate_pct (3+ goal margin) per tournament
- Annotation callout: "Not statistically significant (p=0.40,
  Mann-Whitney U, rank-biserial r=-0.06)" pulled from
  STATISTICAL_VALIDATION — 2026's mean is slightly higher (1.56 vs ~1.39
  pooled), stated as directional only, not causal

--- Page 3 — Upset Rate & Predictability ---
Story beat: does the bigger field produce more shocks?
- Bar: upset_rate_pct per tournament (2026: 16.3%, 2022: 26.5%, 1994:
  26.8% — current live numbers post-backfill)
- Stacked bar or secondary series: draw_rate_pct alongside it
- Annotation callout: "Not statistically significant (p=0.14, Fisher's
  exact, Cohen's h=-0.26)" — sample sizes (eligible/decisive match
  counts) shown directly since they now differ meaningfully from a
  pre-cross-check draft (2026: 80 decisive matches, was 63 before the
  ranking backfill — worth a one-line footnote on data completeness, not
  hidden)

--- Page 4 — Draw Fairness (2026 only) ---
Story beat: is the group stage itself balanced?
- Bar/heatmap: avg_fifa_ranking per group (12 groups, A-L), lower =
  harder group
- Data-completeness note: teams_counted vs teams_in_group column shown
  directly — now that the FIFA ranking backfill (issue #39) filled all 9
  previously-missing values, this should read 4/4 for every group; worth
  confirming live before building
- Explicit label: "2026 only — no verified group data exists for
  2022/1994 in this project"

--- Page 5 — Does Ranking Predict Finish? ---
Story beat: the most direct test of whether seeding still "works" under
the new format.
- Scatter plot: fifa_ranking (x) vs furthest_stage_order/matches_played
  (y), one point per team, faceted or filterable by tournament
  (EXPECTED_VS_ACTUAL)
- Annotation per tournament: Spearman's rho and significance (2026:
  rho=-0.64, n=48, p<0.0001; 2022: rho=-0.48, n=32, p=0.006; 1994:
  rho=-0.61, n=24, p=0.002) — all significant, all in the expected
  direction
- Mandatory caveat banner: 2026 uses real stage_order (load-bearing);
  2022/1994 use a match-count proxy (directional only) — must not be
  visually implied as equal-precision (e.g., don't put them on an
  identical unqualified axis without the distinction called out)

--- Page 6 — The Real Cost: Travel & Rest (2026 only) ---
Story beat: the honest complication — this is what expansion actually
costs, operationally, even though competitive balance held up.
- Histogram or box plot: distance_km distribution (0-4302.7 km) and
  rest_days distribution (3-8 days, mean ~5.3)
- Map or scatter: distance vs rest_days per match, maybe colored by
  confederation
- Framing note: no historical comparison possible (2022/1994 have no
  venue data in this project) — this page argues the logistics cost
  qualitatively, not against a "was it worse before" baseline

--- Page 7 — Confederation Performance ---
Story beat: did expansion (more confederation slots) translate into
genuine competitiveness, or just more early exits?
- Bar: win_rate per confederation per tournament (small multiples or
  filter by tournament)
- Bar: avg_goal_diff per confederation
- Annotation: "Significant, medium effect (Kruskal-Wallis p=0.0009,
  eps^2=0.079)" for 2026 — does not say which confederations differ from
  which, stated explicitly on the page
- Sample-size caveat: OFC n=3 matches in 2026 — visually flag small-n
  confederations rather than let a bar chart imply equal confidence

--- Page 8 (appendix) — Methodology & Confidence ---
Story beat: closes the loop — nothing on this dashboard is asserted
without its statistical backing visible somewhere.
- Table: every row of STATISTICAL_VALIDATION — hypothesis, test used,
  sample size, statistic, p-value, effect size, interpretation sentence
- Serves as the "show your work" page a skeptical analyst would want
  before trusting pages 2-7

===============================================================================
5. WHAT'S DELIBERATELY EXCLUDED, AND WHY
===============================================================================

- Tactical efficiency — cut entirely (issue #37, no-go). Not a page, not
  a footnote metric, gone from scope.
- 2022/1994 group difficulty, travel/rest — no data exists; not faked
  with a proxy the way expected-vs-actual uses one. These pages are
  explicitly 2026-only rather than silently omitted.
- Any "expansion caused X" framing — every page's language is "associated
  with," matching docs/problem_statement.md's stance rule.
